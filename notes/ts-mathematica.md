# TS-Mathematica

A specialized repository combining TypeScript and Wolfram Language for
advanced financial analysis and statistical modeling.

## 📈 Stock Market Education

- **Stock Analysis**:
  - `data/stock-lessons/StockAnalysis.nb`: A comprehensive notebook designed to
    teach or perform stock market analysis.
  - `generate_nb.wl`: A script likely used to programmatically generate
    analysis notebooks.

## 🧪 Statistical Inference

- **P-Value Modeling**:
  - `data/p-value-guess/`: Multiple drafts (`draft1.wl` to `draft4.wls`)
    exploring the logic of p-value estimation and interpretation.
  - `data/p-values/compute.wl`: The core logic for p-value calculations.

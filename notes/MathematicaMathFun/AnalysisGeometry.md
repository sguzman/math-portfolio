# Analysis & Geometry

Classic mathematical analysis and geometry investigations, often focused on
visualization and symbolic manipulation.

## 📐 Complex Analysis

- `complex-fun.wls`: Visualizing complex functions using domain coloring and
  other techniques.
- `complex_nums_to_iter_unc.wls`: Connecting complex numbers to uncertainty in
  iterative processes.
- `what_even_are_complex_nums.wls`: Foundational explorations into the nature of
  complex numbers and their critical points.

## 🎢 Geometry & Spectral Theory

- `curvy-curves.wls`: Curvature, torsion, and other properties of parametric
  curves.
- `spectral_theory.wls`: Exploring eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and spectral
  decompositions, often visualized through Lissajous-style plots.
- `laplace.wls` & `laplace-again.wls`: Working with Laplace transforms and
  differential equations.
- `calculus-of-var.wls`: Solving problems in the calculus of variations.

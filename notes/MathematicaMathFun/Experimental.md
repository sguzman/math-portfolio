# The Lab (Experimental)

A collection of scripts that are either in progress, highly experimental, or
serve as a "graveyard" for ideas that didn't quite pan out but remain
interesting for reference.

## 🧪 Miscellaneous & Experimental

- **Experimental Logic**:
  - `why-isnt-it-working.wls`: Debugging scripts for complex symbolic logic.
  - `wtf-even-is-this.wls`: Random explorations that lack a clear category.
  - `what_am_i_even_doing.wls`: Notes to self in code form.
- **Assorted Tools**:
  - `math_and_stuff.wls`
  - `just_some_fun.wls`
  - `random_math_stuff_idk.wls`
  - `oh-well.wls`
  - `ratata.wls`
- **Utility/Unfinished**:
  - `symbol-mania.wls`
  - `tick_tock.wls`
  - `units-as-context.wls`

# Iterative Dynamics & Functional Calculus

This section focuses on the study of function iteration, discrete dynamical
systems, and the generalization of calculus to iterated operators.

## 🔬 Core Research

- **Geometric Interpretation**:
  - `iteration_as_rotation.wls`: Models the growth of linear functions under
    iteration as a rotation in a specific space.
  - `iteration_rotation.wls`: Further refinement of the rotation model.
- **Algebraic Structures**:
  - `iteration_as_linear_trans.wls`: Treating iteration as a linear
    transformation on function space.
  - `iteration_as_complex_algebra.wls`: Mapping iterative processes to complex
    number operations.
- **Iterated Calculus**:
  - `iterated_calculus.wls`: Exploring derivatives and integrals of iterated
    functions.
  - `iterated_arith_as_complex_num.wls`: Bridging arithmetic sequences and
    complex mappings.

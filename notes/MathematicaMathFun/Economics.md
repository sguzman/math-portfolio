# Economic Modeling & Data Analysis

This section utilizes Mathematica's curated data sources to analyze global
economic trends, inflation, and corporate structures.

## 📈 Economic Analysis

- **Inflation & GDP**:
  - `gdp_by_1950_us_dollars.wls`: Normalizing GDP data to constant 1950 dollars.
  - `more-inflation-computation.wls`: Advanced models for calculating purchasing
    power over time.
  - `inflation_and_gdp_over_time.wls`: Correlation analysis between inflation
    rates and GDP growth.
- **Sector Analysis**:
  - `sector_analysis.wls`: Broad analysis of economic sectors.
  - `sector_percent_analysis.nb`: Detailed breakdown of sector contributions to
    total economy.

## 🏢 Corporate & Financial Data

- `comp-prop-analysis.nb`: Leveraging `Entity` data to analyze company
  properties and industry clusters.
- `pie-charts.wls`: Visualization tools for financial distributions.
- `stats.wls`: General statistical utility scripts used across different
  analyses.

# MathematicaMathFun

This repository contains my core Mathematica document files, primarily focused
on research and exploring mathematical concepts through computation.

## 🔑 Key Areas

- **Calculus of Variations**: Exploration of functionals and extremals.
- **Complex Analysis**: Visualizing complex-valued functions and residues.
- **Differential Geometry**: Curvature analysis and manifold visualizations.

## 📝 Highlighted Files

- **Core Math**:
  - `calculus-of-var.wls`: Scripts for solving variational problems.
  - `complex-fun.wls`: Visualization of complex functions.
  - `curvy-curves.wls`: Investigations into curve properties.
- **Economic/Data Analysis**:
  - `comp-prop-analysis.nb`: Analysis of company data and industries using
    Wolfram entities.
  - `sector_analysis.wls` & `sector_percent_analysis.nb`: Deep dives into
    economic sector performance.
  - `data_analysis.wls`: General data processing and visualization.
- **General Exploration**:
  - `math_and_stuff.wls`: Miscellaneous mathematical curiosities.
  - `stats.wls`: Statistical analysis tools.

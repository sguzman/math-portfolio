# MathematicaMathFun

This repository contains my core Mathematica document files, primarily focused on research and exploring mathematical concepts through computation.

## 🔑 Key Areas

- **Calculus of Variations**: Exploration of functionals and extremals.
- **Complex Analysis**: Visualizing complex-valued functions and residues.
- **Differential Geometry**: Curvature analysis and manifold visualizations.

## 📝 Highlighted Files

- `calculus-of-var.wls`: Scripts for solving variational problems.
- `complex-fun.wls`: Visualization of complex functions.
- `curvy-curves.wls`: Investigations into curve properties.

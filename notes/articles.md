# Articles

`projects/articles` is a writing and idea repository rather than a narrowly mathematical one. It collects treatise drafts, research wishlists, reading agendas, and domain notes. Within that broader writing project, mathematics is still a major thread: several files record math research directions, mathematical self-assessments, and article ideas that overlap with the more technical repos in this portfolio.

## Scope

- **Primary medium**: Markdown notes.
- **Repository style**: compact note lists rather than finished longform manuscripts.
- **Current structure**: one `README.md` plus six note files under `projects/articles/notes`.
- **Role in this portfolio**: conceptual and editorial companion to the more technical math repositories.

## What the repo contains

### Mathematical idea backlog

The strongest connection to this portfolio is `projects/articles/notes/math.md`. It is a dense research backlog of mathematical problems, conjectural formalisms, and possible article directions. The topics overlap heavily with `MathematicaMathFun`, `math-research`, and `MathWhiteBoard`, especially in these areas:

- Collatz and operator-theoretic iteration
- curvature as information or control
- complex-number models of real functions
- generalized function iteration and fractional application
- homotopy, topology, and connectedness
- integral transforms, convolution, and Laplacian-based deformation
- symbolic compression and normal forms for expressions
- alternative coordinate systems and geometric computation

This file is less a polished paper outline and more a raw registry of mathematical ambitions and independently developed intuitions.

### Treatise pipeline

`projects/articles/notes/treatise.md` is the main editorial queue. It mixes completed and planned essays across mathematics, politics, epistemology, culture, economics, and software. For this portfolio, the important point is that some mathematical work appears here not as calculations but as article candidates, including:

- closed-form polynomial iteration
- algebraic interpretation frameworks
- recursion and infinite self-reference
- Collatz via semigroups of bounded linear operators
- symbolic overload, math anxiety, and pedagogy
- open-problem counts as a measure of formal strength

This makes the repo valuable as a map of where formal mathematical work may later turn into essays or standalone research pieces.

### Math self-positioning

`projects/articles/notes/math-brags.md` is short but useful. It records a few ideas the repo owner considers independently discovered or especially representative, such as:

- linear homotopy
- multiplicative versus additive mixture
- dot-product-based functional resonance

This file is not a proof document; it is a compact statement of mathematical identity and recurring motifs.

### Cross-disciplinary research lists

The rest of the repo is broader but still relevant context:

- `projects/articles/notes/cs.md` records computer science directions, especially type systems, macro systems, compile-time reasoning, and formal grammar ideas.
- `projects/articles/notes/to-research.md` is a wider reading and research queue spanning operating systems, programming languages, formal systems, and math topics like complex analysis, functional analysis, and spectral theory.
- `projects/articles/notes/reading-stuff.md` is a generalized reading agenda, including math textbooks, journals, and research papers.

These files matter because they explain the conceptual inputs feeding the math work elsewhere in the portfolio.

## File inventory

- `projects/articles/README.md`: top-level description of the repository as a treatise and note collection.
- `projects/articles/notes/math.md`: the main mathematical research backlog and idea ledger.
- `projects/articles/notes/math-brags.md`: short list of mathematical ideas and self-assessed original insights.
- `projects/articles/notes/treatise.md`: master list of drafted and planned treatises, including several math-adjacent essays.
- `projects/articles/notes/cs.md`: computer science idea backlog, especially around formal systems and type theory.
- `projects/articles/notes/to-research.md`: research wishlist spanning CS, mathematics, and technical infrastructure.
- `projects/articles/notes/reading-stuff.md`: reading agenda and content taxonomy.

## Why it belongs in the math portfolio

Even though `projects/articles` is not exclusively mathematical, it belongs here for three reasons:

- it preserves the high-level mathematical agenda behind the code-heavy repos,
- it captures article-scale interpretations of ideas that appear technically elsewhere,
- and it records the speculative research vocabulary that ties together iteration, symbolic systems, topology, operators, and computation.

In practice, this repo functions as the conceptual notebook sitting above the more formal mathematical experiments.

# Mathematica Data

A repository dedicated to storing and processing computational results,
primarily focused on economic indicators and correlation studies.

## 📊 Economic Datasets

- **OEC (Observatory of Economic Complexity)**:
  - Scripts like `eci-get.wls` and `pci-get-hs4.wls` fetch data on economic
    complexity and product complexity.
  - CSV files contain mappings for countries and products (HS4, HS6).
- **Inflation & GDP**:
  - `correlation-sp500-ranking/`: Studies the relationship between S&P 500
    performance and various rankings.
  - `kraft-heinz-vs-verisign/`: A specific correlation study between KHC and
    VRSN stocks using `correlate-khc-vrsn.wls`.

## 👥 Social & Historical Data

- **Pantheon People 2020**:
  - `person_2020_update.csv`: Cleaned data regarding globally significant
    individuals, likely for network or demographic analysis.
